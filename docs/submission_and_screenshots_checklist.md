## 2. Screenshot Checklist

For the report and viva defense, verify the following screenshots are captured:

1. **Splash Screen**: Initial logo displaying local session load animations.
2. **Onboarding Carousel**: Multi-slide setup introducing features.
3. **Login & Register Forms**: Form fields displaying client-side validation errors.
4. **Dashboard Home**: Interactive wellness cards and climate widgets.
5. **Mood Logging**: Sliders for stress levels, sleep, and water intake.
6. **Habit Recommendations**: Explained recommendations showing top SHAP factors.
7. **AI Coach Chat**: Compassionate chat box displaying medical disclaimers.
8. **Reports Trends**: fl_chart plots for burnout risks and mood metrics.
9. **Settings Configurations**: Theme selections, font scale adjustments, and profile settings.
10. **Data management panel**: PDF backup export confirmations.
11. **Offline Warning Banner**: The orange warning banner displayed when offline.
12. **Double Confirmation Dialog**: The account deletion confirmation prompt.
